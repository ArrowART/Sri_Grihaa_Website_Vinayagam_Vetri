About Us

We Are a Powerful Modern Team in Renovating

Sri Grihaa Builders stands out as the Best Architects in Coimbatore and the Top Interior Designers in Coimbatore, offering innovative and bespoke architectural and interior design solutions. As a subsidiary of the renowned Monnaie Architects & Interiors, headquartered in Palakkad, we bring over 15+ years of experience in transforming spaces into functional and visually stunning environments. With a proven track record of successfully executing 5,000+ projects across South India—including key cities like Coimbatore, Chennai, Kochi, Thrissur, Palakkad, Salem, Munnar, and Bangalore—we are trusted by clients for our unwavering commitment to quality and customer satisfaction.
